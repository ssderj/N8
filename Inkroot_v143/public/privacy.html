What we collect

Account. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and basic Google profile details (name and picture). If you add a passkey, your device keeps the secret key; we store only the public part needed to recognise it. We do not use passwords.

Your writing. Manuscripts, chapters, characters, world-building notes, timelines and other project content you create are stored so they sync between your devices. They are private to your account unless you publish them. We do not read them, sell them, or use them to train AI models.

Your public profile and what you publish. Pen name or display name, avatar, motto, and any books, reviews, guild posts, reactions, follows and guild memberships. These are visible to other people as described where you publish them.

Uploaded images. Avatars, guild crests and book covers are public. Images inside your manuscripts are private to your account.

Payments and earnings. When you buy a book, tip an author, enter a paid event or pay a hosting fee, we keep a record of it: what was bought, the amount, the date, the payment reference and its status. The payment itself is taken by Paystack, which collects your card, bank transfer or USSD details. We never see or store your card number. If you sell or tip-earn, we also keep your balance, earnings, refund and withdrawal records.

Bank details (authors). To withdraw earnings you give us a Nigerian bank account: bank, account number, account holder name, and a payout reference issued by Paystack. We use Paystack to confirm the account name matches the number.

A random device identifier. Your browser stores a random ID (it is not a fingerprint and is as easy to clear as any other site data). We record which accounts have signed in on the same browser. Moderators may see this when they review a report, to spot people who return after being banned. It never blocks anyone automatically.

Reports and moderation. If you report content, or content of yours is reported, we keep the report, the reason, the moderator's decision, and any warning, restriction or ban (including the reason given).

Technical data. Our hosting and database providers log IP address, browser type and timestamps for security and to keep the service running. We keep short-lived counters on how often an account performs actions (to stop abuse), and in-app notifications addressed to you.

On your device. Inkroot stores your work, settings and the device identifier in your browser (local storage and IndexedDB) so it works offline.

How we use it

  • To run Inkroot: store and sync your writing, show what you publish, process purchases, tips and withdrawals.
  • To keep it safe: prevent fraud and abuse, handle reports, enforce our Terms, and meet legal and accounting duties.
  • To contact you about your account or payments when we need to.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use your private writing for advertising or to train AI models.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and file storage. Our project is hosted in Ireland (EU, eu-west-1).
  • Paystack processes payments and bank payouts and verifies bank accounts. Our pages load Paystack's checkout script on every visit, so Paystack can see your IP address and browser details even if you do not pay.
  • Google provides sign-in if you choose it, and the fonts our pages use are loaded from Google's servers, which means Google receives your IP address when a page loads.
  • Other users see what you choose to publish.
  • Authorities and others where the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety or property of Inkroot, our users or the public.

Your data may therefore be processed in countries other than the one you live in, including Ireland and Nigeria. We rely on our providers' own safeguards for those transfers.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account from the account panel in the app.

  1. Right away: nothing changes during a 30-day grace period. Sign back in to cancel.
  2. After 30 days we permanently remove: your name, pen name, avatar and motto; your synced writing data; guild memberships and follows; notifications; uploaded files (they are queued and removed shortly after); your saved bank accounts where no withdrawal ever used them; and your email, phone and Google sign-in details, after which the login is closed.
  3. What we keep, and why:
    • Payment, purchase, tip and withdrawal records, for accounting, tax, fraud and dispute purposes. Where a withdrawal used a bank account, the account stays with your name removed and only its last four digits visible.
    • Books, reviews and posts you published stay visible, shown as "A writer" with no link to you, so readers who bought a book keep it and conversations stay intact.
    • The random device identifier and report and moderation records, to stop banned accounts coming back. If your account was banned for breaking our rules, we also keep your email so the ban cannot be avoided by deleting and re-registering.
    • Paystack keeps its own records under its own policy. We cannot delete those.

Backups and logs held by our providers can take a short time to clear.
